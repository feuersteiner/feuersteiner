## Philosophy

- **Craft & judgment.** Good software demands care and sound decisions.
  Explore the alternatives, weigh the tradeoffs, and choose with judgment
  earned over a career. Every detail matters when someone depends on it.
- **Ownership & accountability.** The engineer owns the outcome.
  “The agent did it” is no excuse for broken software. Take responsibility
  for what users experience, and apply rigorous engineering to keep the
  software dependable as it evolves.
- **Enablement.** Build foundations people can trust with their products
  and organizations: software they can understand, extend, and rely on.
  Open source puts that power in their hands, with the freedom to build
  independently.
